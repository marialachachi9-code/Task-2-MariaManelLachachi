About PeakFit

PeakFit opened in 2018 with one goal: make a gym people actually want to go to. We keep our equipment modern, our classes small, and our coaches close by.

Whether you're lifting for the first time or training for a competition, our team will help you build a plan that fits your goals and your schedule.
